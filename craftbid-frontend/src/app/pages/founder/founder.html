THE STORY BEHIND CRAFTBID

From Inspiration to a Live Digital Marketplace

Growing up with an appreciation for India's immense cultural depth and traditional craftsmanship, Ranjit Barik recognized that while the digital commerce revolution swept the nation, traditional artisans remained left behind due to complex portals, exorbitant listing fees, and opaque commission structures.

Ranjit set out to build an end-to-end digital ecosystem combining three powerful pillars:

  • 🎥
    Engaging Visual Storytelling: Craft Reels that let artisans upload short-form videos showcasing how their art is molded, painted, or woven.
  • ⏱️
    Fair & Thrilling Live Auctions: A 60-second real-time turn auction system with a pay-to-bid base deposit and differential bidding that ensures authentic bids.
  • 🛡️
    100% Guaranteed Escrow & Refunds: Instant automated deposit reversals for non-winning bidders and guaranteed 90% direct payouts for artisans.

Today, CraftBid stands as a modern, high-performance platform engineered from scratch with clean, scalable, cloud-native architecture.
